# Topic labeling guidelines (single label, agent-labeled by Claude Opus, pending human review)

The topic is the subject matter, independent of country (Panama relevance is the R component).
- economia: prices, inflation, consumers (Acodeco), employment, wages, pensions and their finances, public budget and public finances, taxes, subsidies, investment, trade, business, mining, agricultural markets.
- logistica_canal: Panama Canal operation and administration, ports, maritime authority and ships, shipping, bunkering, logistics, cargo.
- turismo: tourism, tourists, cruises, hotels, tourism promotion and tourism policy.
- servicios_publicos: water supply, electricity (tariffs, outages), health services (CSS, Minsa, hospitals, epidemiology), public education services (schools, scholarships), public transport, roads and public works, waste collection, public paperwork services.
- eventos_naturales: weather, rain, storms, floods, landslides, earthquakes, droughts, hurricanes, El Niño, disaster preparedness and civil protection response.
- regulacion: laws, bills, decrees, technical norms, regulators' rules, legislative approval of norms, court rulings on norms, institutional reforms.
- sin_tema: sports, entertainment, celebrities, crime and police, individual judicial cases, accidents and fires, culture and festivals, geopolitics and foreign politics, lottery, human interest, media self-promotion.
- Budgets of a sector agency (ACP, AMP) go to that sector's topic; the national budget process goes to economia.
