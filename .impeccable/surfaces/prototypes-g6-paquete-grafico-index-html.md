# Editable Borrador prototype

Mode: Operate.
Scope: Design-only extension of the existing approved Paquete gráfico prototype.
Keep the production app, backend, and shared prototype data unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Put the editable newsroom document first, with evidence-aware assistance alongside it rather than on a separate Consultas page.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the existing Barlow typography, navy newsroom header, blue actions, yellow selection cues, white document, and cool gray workspace.

STORY: An editor changes the title and draft, asks a suggested Spanish question, inspects sources, and explicitly applies or dismisses a proposed edit.
All assistant behavior is demo-only and edits are not persisted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact agenda summary replaces the large lead card while editing.
The ranked list stays left, the editable document occupies the middle, and a docked assistant keeps replies above its bottom composer.
Mobile uses the document alone with an expandable assistant sheet.

FORM: Local extension of the approved existing master-detail layout, confirmed by the user.
No concept seed applies to this narrow extension.
The signature interaction is applying a proposed title and highlighting the edited document field.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
